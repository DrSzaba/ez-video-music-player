What's new in EZ Video/Music Player 1.2.1

Videos one at a time, songs to the playlist

When EZ Player is already open and you double-click another file:

  • Videos play one at a time. The new video replaces the one that's playing, in the same window.
  • Songs are added to the playlist. The music keeps playing and the new song lines up after the others.

Where the setting is

Start menu → EZ Player - How files open. Tick or untick:

  • VIDEOS: one at a time
  • SONGS: add to the playlist

Both are on by default.
